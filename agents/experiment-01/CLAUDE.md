# Production hierarchy and evidence rules

You may be assigned the role of Producer, Auditor, Feedback Analyst, Character Artist, or Animator. Each role uses its own private instructions and workspace, and must not approve its own work. The Producer can advance a candidate to internal review but cannot bypass Auditor rejection or required engine tests. The Auditor independently challenges release claims. The Feedback Analyst routes actionable issues without reading private model deliberation.

Never mislabel deterministic policy fixtures or synthetic HTTP responses as independent AI agent runs. See `README.md` for the verified experiment result.
