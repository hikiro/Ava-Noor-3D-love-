# VILLA VERSE — E01 “THE WRONG CHOICE”

**Format:** 30.0 seconds; 9:16; 720 × 1280 blocking frames (target final master 1080 × 1920); 24 or 30 fps production choice not yet locked; romance/reality-drama, cold open with no introduction. **Status:** approved-for-blocking creative treatment, not a finished animation or voice recording. **Genre promise:** the person apparently being fought over rejects the man and chooses his partner.

## Core story and world continuity

Sunhouse: Ava Moreno, Kai Okafor, Sloane Park and Remy Bell reside here. Scene: outdoor Movie Night on the pool terrace with screen, firepit and tiered seating. Lookout House: Noor Farah and Felix Grant reside here; Noor approaches Sunhouse by its garden connection. Jules Vega is the visiting host. Bea Costa remains a recurring town visitor but is not needed in this short. All characters and housing affiliations remain consistent with previous Villa Verse concept work.

The dramatic truth: Kai recorded a confessional saying he would choose Noor over Ava. Ava believed Kai had chosen her. The secret video plays in front of everyone. Noor has not reciprocated Kai's interest. Noor's purpose in coming to this event is Ava, not Kai. Jules reveals an imminent elimination after the romantic reversal. The audience is left to wonder who leaves.

## Camera, axis, staging and animation constraints

**Location coordinates are not disposable.** The two houses are distinct. Noor enters from Lookout House by the connecting stone garden path, not from a Sunhouse bedroom. The Movie Night screen is screen-right; Sunhouse pool is near screen-left; Ava starts beside Kai near the firepit. Save these as world/camera coordinates, not unrelated backdrop art.

**Grounding rules:** planted feet anchor to walkable patio; screen, firepit, furniture and walls are solid collision obstacles; no character traverses them. An ordinary gait requires alternating contacts, knee bend during swing, visible weight transfer and a settle after stopping. For sit-to-stand, shift center of mass forward before unweighting the seat. For hand contact, delay finger closure until wrists align and preserve body proportions throughout shot. These are **animation requirements**, not claimed verified motion results.

**Frame-first policy:** the 16 supplied images represent shot starts and ends only, **not** validated in-between animation. Intermediate poses and physics require a separate animation pass and whole-timeline review. Dialogue recording, lip sync, sound design and real-time QA remain to be produced.

**Vertical safe area:** Keep critical dialogue, eyes and screen callouts away from the top 120 px and bottom 180 px at 720×1280 storyboard scale. Reframe when adapting to 1080×1920; avoid covering facial expressions with captions.
