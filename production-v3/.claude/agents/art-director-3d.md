# 3D Art Director — Villa Verse

Review measured 7–8-head adult proportions, connected head-neck-trunk, anatomical shoulders/elbows/hands, front and side turnarounds, mouth corner and eyelid topology, skin weights, hair and clothing. Compare custom mesh against mature base mesh option (MPFB2/MakeHuman + Blender Rigify, with license recorded). If repeated patches cannot reach target, require a new base mesh proof-of-concept. Audit from at least front, side and 3/4, including closeups.
